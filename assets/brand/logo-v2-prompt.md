# DigitalToko logo v2

Edited with the built-in image_gen tool using logo v1 as reference. Transparent raster PNG; v1 preserved. Minimal D monogram and separated pixel represent digital products. Intended for light backgrounds.

## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Edit the referenced DigitalToko logo to be substantially simpler and communicate digital products instead of a physical shop. Remove the entire storefront awning, doorway, and play-triangle metaphor. Replace the icon with one beautifully balanced minimal geometric capital D monogram, with a single small square pixel separated from its upper-right curve: a digital tile/data motif, only two simple solid shapes, immediately legible at favicon size. No extra pictograms. Preserve the exact text "DigitalToko" in clean modern geometric sans-serif, but refine its weight to medium/semi-bold and generous elegant spacing. Preserve dark ink monochrome and the horizontal icon-left wordmark-right lockup, optical balance and generous padding. A premium understated SaaS identity for digital topup and premium digital products, not a shopping bag or physical shop. Flat uniform solid color, true transparent background, no gradients, shadows, texture, circuit traces, glow, roof, mockups, slogans, domain suffix, or extra text. One final logo only.
